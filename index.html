Capture tips

Orbit rings (guided)

Shoot three height bands around the object: Low, Eye, and High. Aim for ~8–12 photos per ring (about 24–36 total) for small detailed parts like tool ports and adapters.

Overlap & framing

  • ~60% overlap between neighboring shots
  • Keep the object filling most of the frame
  • Hold steady; tap shutter, then move a little

Setup

  • Matte / non-reflective background (paper, cloth)
  • Even diffuse light — avoid hard specular glare
  • Optional: lazy susan / turntable for consistent orbits
  • ArUco markers in view help scale later on PC

What this app does / doesn’t

Captures high-res stills and exports a ZIP for photogrammetry on your computer (COLMAP / Meshroom). It does not build a mesh on-device. iPhone 12 has no rear LiDAR, and TrueDepth is not available to Safari.

Export

Packs JPEGs + scan.json into a ZIP named scan-YYYYMMDD-HHMM.zip. On iPhone, prefer Share (Files / AirDrop) — Safari downloads can be flaky.
